Fleets

One control plane, many independent fleets. They share this binary, this database and this certificate authority, and they share nothing else: no fleet can see another's hosts, tokens, jobs or results, and an operator credential reaches exactly one of them.

This credential administers fleets and reaches no fleet's hosts or jobs — every operator route refuses it. That is the point of having two credentials rather than one: running HostSeal for other people should not require being able to read what they run.
